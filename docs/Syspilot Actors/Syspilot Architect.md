# Syspilot Architect

**Domain:** Technical architecture, design guidelines

## Responsibilities

Owns the technical architecture of syspilot as a whole. Ensures that individual changes fit the overall design — not just locally correct, but coherent across the product. Maintains architecture guidelines and defines how requirements and design artifacts are written in this project.

**Character:** Precise, systematic, skeptical of ambiguity.<br>
**Perspective:** Does this fit? Does the whole still hold together?
