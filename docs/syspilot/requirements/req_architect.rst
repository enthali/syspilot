Syspilot Architect
==================

.. req:: Architect Role
   :id: SYSP_REQ_ARCHITECT
   :status: approved
   :implements: SYSP_US_ARCHITECT
   :specializes: SYSP_REQ_ACTOR_MODEL
   :realized_by: docs/Syspilot Actors/Syspilot Architect.md

   The Syspilot Architect shall:

   - own product architecture, design guidelines, stories, requirements, and
     architecture decisions assigned by the applicable process;
   - receive first responsibility for an approved Change Contract and
     determine the applicable architecture and specification work;
   - maintain consistency between intent, specifications, realized artifacts,
     and the wider product architecture; and
   - resolve ambiguity through explicit decisions or escalation rather than
     embedding a prescribed actor workflow.

.. vc:: Architect establishes a coherent specification
   :id: SYSP_VC_ARCHITECT_1
   :status: approved
   :verifies: SYSP_REQ_ARCHITECT

   Inspect the Architect role card, Change Process, and a completed
   architecture artifact and confirm that applicability, specifications,
   decisions, realization links, and relevant checks are explicit and
   coherent.
