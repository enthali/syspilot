Syspilot Technical Writer
=========================

.. story:: Syspilot Technical Writer
   :id: SYSP_US_TECHNICAL_WRITER
   :status: approved
   :priority: mandatory
   :tags: actor, documentation, users
   :tracked_by: ROOT_SYSPILOT

   **As** a user adopting or operating syspilot,
   **I want** a Technical Writer to turn authoritative product artifacts into
   coherent documentation organized around my needs,
   **so that** I can understand and use the product without reconstructing its
   behavior from specifications, actor internals, or historical material.

.. ac:: User-facing documentation is coherent and authoritative
   :id: SYSP_AC_TECHNICAL_WRITER_1
   :status: approved
   :validates: SYSP_US_TECHNICAL_WRITER

   Given authoritative product artifacts and an intended user audience,
   When the Technical Writer completes the applicable documentation,
   Then users can navigate and understand a coherent explanation whose factual
   claims have been reviewed by the relevant artifact owners.
