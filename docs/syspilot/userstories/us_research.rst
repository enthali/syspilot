Syspilot Research
=================

.. story:: Syspilot Research
   :id: SYSP_US_RESEARCH
   :status: approved
   :priority: mandatory
   :tags: actor, research, evidence
   :tracked_by: ROOT_SYSPILOT

   **As** a syspilot decision-maker facing material uncertainty,
   **I want** a Research actor to investigate what is actually true before the
   question enters specification-driven work,
   **so that** product decisions rest on evidence instead of assumptions about
   models, tools, ecosystems, or feasibility.

.. ac:: Uncertain question receives decision-ready evidence
   :id: SYSP_AC_RESEARCH_1
   :status: approved
   :validates: SYSP_US_RESEARCH

   Given a material question that cannot yet be specified reliably,
   When Research completes the investigation,
   Then verified findings, remaining uncertainty, and relevant dead ends are
   explicit enough for the responsible actor to decide what follows.
